# Publication note — 23 November 2026

Before publication, the manuscript (`MANUSCRIPT.tex`) was given a full
read-through and contradiction check, the same standard applied to the
author's other papers. The mathematics was re-derived and is unchanged:
no finding, number, or claim in the paper was altered. Six wording
contradictions were found and fixed in `MANUSCRIPT.tex`:

1. The periodic axiom-print paragraph listed four theorems and then said
   "these five." The fifth declaration printed in that run (E73) was the
   whole-space comparator theorem. The sentence now says the four periodic
   declarations joined the whole-space four, and that the same run printed
   the whole-space comparator theorem as well.
2. The bibliography gave the audit log's range as entries E0–E77. The log
   runs to E88. Fixed.
3. A remark in the anisotropy section called the viscous term "a sum of
   three homogeneous pieces with distinct degrees." The paper's own
   proposition gives two weights, because the two radial pieces share the
   degree b−1. Now "three homogeneous pieces of two distinct degrees."
4. The conclusion said "neither degree is the isotropic weight b−1 except
   at h=0." The radial degree is b−1 at every admissible h. Now "the axial
   degree reaches the isotropic weight b−1 only at h=0."
5. The scope section said the audit "does not re-run the nine external
   checkers." Comparator was run and passes on both challenges (E75, E79),
   as the introduction states. Now "the other eight external checkers."
6. The abstract and the conclusion said the artifact verifies "zero
   axioms" / "there are no axioms," while the paper itself reports that
   `#print axioms` gives `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
   Both now say "no declared axioms," which is the claim the evidence
   supports: the repository declares no axioms of its own.

The `MANUSCRIPT.pdf` in this repository was regenerated by the manual `typeset`
workflow (`.github/workflows/typeset.yml`) after these six fixes, and the
corrected PDF is the one submitted for publication. `CAVEMAN.pdf` and
`LAYMAN.pdf` are built by the same workflow.
